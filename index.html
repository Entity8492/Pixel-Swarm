Pixel Swarm - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The short version

Pixel Swarm does not collect, store or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data saved on your device

Your progress, characters, settings and records are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Online multiplayer

Online co-op is optional. When you host or join a room, your device connects directly to the other players' devices. To set up that connection, the game uses the free PeerJS connection service (peerjs.com) and Google's public STUN server, which see your device's IP address while connecting. The other players in your room see your hero's name and appearance and receive game data such as your position, during that session only. Pixel Swarm has no chat, and nothing from a session is stored by us.

Permissions

The game uses internet access (for online multiplayer only) and vibration (for controller and touch feedback). It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Pixel Swarm does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions? Contact the developer through the Pixel Swarm page: zenerationzero.itch.io/pixel-swarm.
